## What This Vintage Taught

1. **Read the lot sheet before ordering.** Both musts arrived at pH 4.1. Oak Barrel published it in August. High-potassium fruit — tartaric was the wrong first tool.
2. **Bench trials are accurate. Carboys are slow.** pH read 30 minutes after an addition ran 0.15–0.22 high. Wait overnight before deciding to add more.
3. **TA sizes the acid addition, not pH.** Use the landing equation with measured TA and malic.
4. **Pick the MLF strain against projected alcohol before chaptalizing.** Sangiovese was chaptalized first; VP41's ceiling became relevant after.
5. **A zero-SO2 window at high pH has a cost.** Sangiovese carried six weeks without adequate SO2 — estimated 35–45 mg/L acetaldehyde load going into aging.
