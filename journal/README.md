The guide lives in [`guide/`](../guide/). The formulas behind the numbers live in [`formulas/`](../formulas/).

## What Goes Here

- Lab readings with timestamps
- What was added, when, and how much
- Bench trial results
- What the wine tasted like at each stage
- What was learned and what changes in the next vintage

**What stays out:** the cellar log and bench trial workbooks stay on your Mac (`~/Documents/Refinery Row/`). Those are the source of truth. This journal is the narrative around them.
